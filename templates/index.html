            How to Enable USB or Wireless Debugging on Phone & Android TV

            1. Open Settings on your Android Phone or Android TV / Google TV Streamer.
            2. Go to About phone / About TV → find Build number (or OS Version) and tap/click it 7 times until you see "You are now a developer!".
            3. Go to Settings → System → Developer options.
            4. For Android TV / Google TV Streamer (Wi-Fi): Enable USB debugging (on Android TV this automatically opens Wi-Fi debugging on port 5555!) and click 🔍 Auto-Discover in our Wireless panel.
            5. For Phones (USB or Wi-Fi): Enable USB debugging (for cable) or Wireless debugging (for Wi-Fi pairing).
