        How to Enable USB or Wireless Debugging on Android

        1. Open Settings on your Android phone (Samsung, Pixel, Xiaomi, OnePlus, Motorola, etc.).
        2. Go to About phone → find Build number (or MIUI / OS Version) and tap it rapidly 7 times until you see "You are now a developer!".
        3. Go to Settings → System / Additional settings → Developer options.
        4. For USB Cable: Enable USB debugging, plug in the cable, and tap Allow on the phone screen.
        5. For Wi-Fi (Cable-Free): Enable Wireless debugging, tap on its text to open the pairing screen, and use the 📶 Wireless ADB panel in this app.
